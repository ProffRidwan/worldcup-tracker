About World Cup 2026 Tracker

Welcome to the World Cup 2026 Live Tracker a free, fast, and mobile-friendly app built to help football fans follow every match of the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

This app provides real-time match scores, group standings, and full fixture lists for all 104 matches across the tournament, covering all 48 participating nations.

Why I Built This

My name is Ridwan, a software engineer based in Nigeria. I built this app during the 2026 World Cup to give football fans especially across Africa a clean, simple, and free tool to follow the tournament without any clutter or distractions.

What You Get

  • ⚽ Live match scores updated automatically
  • 🏆 Group standings for all 12 groups
  • 📋 Full fixture list with matchday filters
  • 📱 Works perfectly on any mobile device
  • 🆓 Completely free — no login, no signup

Tech Stack

Built with Angular 17, powered by the football-data.org API, and deployed on Vercel.

Follow the developer on X (Twitter) for updates and new features throughout the tournament.
